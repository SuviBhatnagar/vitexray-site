Engagement models

Hire the way that fits your team

Three straightforward ways to work with us. We'll help you pick the right one for the role, budget and timeline, and we'll keep the terms plain.

C2C

Corp-to-Corp

Contractors engaged through their own corporation or a partner vendor. Flexible, fast and ideal for project-based or specialized work.

For clients
Add specialized skills quickly without adding headcount.
For vendors
Submit your consultants against our open requirements on clear C2C terms.
W2

W2 Contract

Contract professionals on W2 payroll. You get the talent you need, and we take care of the onboarding and payroll paperwork.

For clients
Simple onboarding with a single invoice, and contract-to-hire options.
For candidates
A steady, transparent W2 engagement with a responsive point of contact.
FTE

Full-time (Direct Hire)

Permanent search for the people you want to keep. We source, screen and shortlist, and you make the hire directly.

For clients
A focused shortlist of candidates matched to your stack and culture.
For candidates
Real, permanent opportunities with honest context about the role.

How we work

A clear path from requirement to start date

  1. 01

    Understand the role

    We start with a short conversation about skills, team, timeline, budget and engagement type, so we're searching for the right person, not just the right keywords.

  2. 02

    Source & screen

    We tap our network and active sourcing, then screen for technical depth, communication, availability and work authorization.

  3. 03

    Shortlist & interview

    You receive a focused shortlist with context on each candidate. We coordinate interviews and collect feedback quickly.

  4. 04

    Onboard & support

    We handle offers, paperwork and onboarding, then stay in touch after the start date to make sure the placement is working.
